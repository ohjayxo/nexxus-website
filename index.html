Who we work with

Is this for your shop?

It’s for you if:

  • You make parts for defense primes, or for companies that supply them.
  • A prime has sent you a supplier questionnaire, a contract clause to follow, or a question about your Supplier Performance Risk System (SPRS) score.
  • Nobody at your shop does compliance full time.
  • You want the paperwork written for you, not a course on the rules.

It’s probably not for you if:

  • You need computers or networks set up. That’s your IT provider’s work.
  • You need an audit or a certification. We don’t do either.
  • You need a legal opinion on a contract.
  • You only handle Federal Contract Information (FCI), not CUI. Our work is built around Level 2.

Not sure? Finding out is what the first call is for.

The process

How it works

Six steps, in order. You can stop after step 3.

Step What happens

  1. Step 1

    You call or email Josh. He asks a few quick questions, then books a free 15-minute call with Juan to see if we’re a fit. If we’re not, we’ll say so.

  2. Step 2
