Where the program stands

What paused, and what didn't.

A reform review of the program is under way. As of our last review, the underlying rules have not changed.

Paused

Phase 2 — third-party certification

On July 13, 2026 the Department suspended third-party (C3PAO) Level 2 certification and DIBCAC Level 3 certification while a reform task force reviews the program.

Still in force

Self-assessments, and the clauses behind them

Level 1 and Level 2 self-assessments with an annual affirmation. DFARS 252.204-7012 safeguarding and 72-hour incident reporting. A posted SPRS score under DFARS 252.204-7019 and -7020. Flow-downs from your prime. Federal enforcement.

Worth knowing

Six things to understand before your next questionnaire.

The failure mode isn't your firewall. It's paperwork, scoping, and the accuracy of what you've said.

Paused isn't gone

The outside assessor is on hold. The DFARS clauses, the posted SPRS score, and the requirements that flow down from your prime are not. Your next solicitation, option year, or prime questionnaire won't wait for Washington.

Enforcement didn't pause

Federal settlements with defense contractors and a major university have turned on inaccurate cybersecurity statements, not on breaches. Getting hacked wasn't the trigger. Saying something inaccurate was.

No paper, no assessment

A Basic Assessment is based on your System Security Plan. Without one, the DoD methodology treats the assessment as one that couldn't be completed. A control you run but never documented is, on paper, a control you don't have.

A plan of action earns no points

A requirement that isn't implemented is scored as not implemented, whether or not a plan exists to fix it. And the score you post comes with the date you say you'll reach 110 — a representation, not a hope.

Old machines aren't disqualifying

Equipment that can't meet a requirement, such as an older machine controller, can be documented as a specialized asset and described, with its mitigations, in the System Security Plan. Age alone isn't a barrier.

Level 1 has no grace period

At Level 1, all 15 requirements (FAR 52.204-21) have to be met before the annual affirmation is signed. A plan of action isn't permitted at any point.
