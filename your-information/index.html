Your information

How your information is handled

  • We never take custody of controlled unclassified information (CUI). Not by email, not on a drive, not even to look at.
  • If your work falls under ITAR, tell us on the first call.
  • We look at where information goes, not at the information itself. We walk your office and floor with a checklist and ask who receives drawings, where files are saved, and what gets emailed. We don’t open, copy, or take drawings or files.
  • Your IT provider keeps the technical work: installing, setting up, and running your systems.

Please don’t send us drawings, contracts, or controlled information, by email or through this site. We don’t need them.
